OBJECTIVES: (1) The overriding objective and central mission of PRO is Human Rights Education, Publication and Litigation in the areas of:

  • (A) Civil, Social, Political, Cultural and Economic Rights as well as Rights to Development.
  • (B) Good Government and Popular Participation.
  • (C) Citizens increased access to Health Care.
  • (D) Poverty Reduction.
  • (E) Women and Children's Rights.
  • (F) Civil society development.
  • (G) Conflict Prevention and Resolution.
  • (H) Labour Rights.
  • (I) Environmental Rights.
  • (J) To further promote understanding, tolerance, gender equality and peaceful co-existence amongst various segments that make up the African society.
  • (L) To advocate and campaign for Legal Reforms to ensure that Laws possess Human Rights content and enable people secure quality life of Dignity.

(2) Offering of Legal Aid and counseling to victims of Human Rights violations and seeking redress on behalf of the Victims as well as instituting Public interest Litigation, PIL, in Courts.

(3) Engaging in Human Rights Publications of Books, Reports, Bulletins etc. for Schools, Human Rights Educators, Law enforcement officials etc.

The objectives shall be carried out via five principal organs, namely; People's Rights Resource and Training Centre (PRRTC), People's Rights Skill Acquisition and Technology Centre (PRSATC), Legal Clinic and Reform Unit (LCRU), Women and Children's Affairs Section (WCAS), and Membership/Special Duties Unit (MSDU).

  1. People's Rights Resources and Training Centre (PRRTC):- Shall (a) Conduct intensive induction courses for all intending members to empower them with basic knowledge of Human Rights and how to make Human Rights a reality in the daily lives of the people as well as exposing them to the basic norms, principles, tenets, and values of Human Rights, powers of the Police & duties of citizens, Tenants Rights etc.
    2. Other functions of PRRTC includes:-
    • (A) To vigorously pursue the inclusion of Human Rights subjects/courses in the curricula of schools, colleges, polytechnics, universities as well as vocational and private schools, Police and Prison colleges, military institutions etc by organizing Human Rights internship programmes in such colleges, schools, universities etc.
    • (B) Engage in Human Rights extension and Consultancy services and making Human Rights Publications & Books and teaching materials available to individuals, schools, tertiary institutions, military, police and prison colleges.
    • (C) Research, document and monitor Human Rights issues, problems, and abuses and breakthroughs including Election monitoring/Observation.
    • (D) Engage also in gender sensitive training of trainers on Human Rights and related matters.
    • (E) Translate Human Rights Materials to culturally adapt to the needs of the target audience.
  2. People's Rights, Skill Acquisition and Technology Centre (PRSATC). PRO considers poverty as a serious violation of the social and economic Rights of the people and shall do the following:-
    • (A) Build the confidence and capacities of the poor, marginalized and disadvantaged to become productive members of the society.
    • (B) To create opportunities for the unemployed and the poor to maximize their potentials and enhance their abilities to participate in a free society.
    • (C) To develop indigenous technology, stimulate entrepreneurship and create jobs.
    • (D) To make available to the poor and unemployed productive resources such as training, technology, education, credit, land etc and avoid wastage of human resources and suppression of their potentials.
    • (E) To give them opportunity to being architect of their own development and become self-reliants as well as enjoy their Rights to development.
  3. Legal Clinic and Reform Unit: (LCRU) This unit is responsible for day to day counseling of the people especially victims or potential victims of Human Rights violations. Matters that require writing of legal letters are also handled here. The unit also take matters to Court both on Public impart matters and those of individuals occasioning threatened or actual breach of the Human Rights provisions in the Constitution. It also handles all matters pertaining to police detention and prisons as well as offering legal aid to Awaiting Trial Persons. Above all, this unit is charged with the responsibility to campaign and advocate for major legal Reforms to ensure that the Laws possess Human Rights content.
  4. Women and Children's Rights Unit (WCRU): This unit would engage in consciousness raising education of members of the society (both men and women) to empower them to appreciate the value of women and respond positively to women's entitlements. It would address issues such as Violence Against Women and the Girl Child, women's political participation, oppression of widows, widowhood practices, cultural and traditional inhibitions against the growth and prosperity of women, women inheritance Rights etc. We shall also adopt Gender Empowerment Measure (GEM) to enhance the status of women as well as gender mainstreaming. The unit shall also handle matters pertaining to street children, neglected children, abused children and child labour by ensuring that children meet their basic learning needs and enjoy Rights to Basic Education as well as counseling of deliquent children and seeking to resettle and rehabilitate them etc.
  5. Membership/Special Duties Units (MSDU): Membership/Special Duties Units (MSDU): This unit will attend to the needs of membership branches and perform other special duties for attainment of the aims and objectives of the Organization.

METHODS/STRATEGY OF IMPLEMENTING THE OBJECTIVES:

  1. Human Rights induction courses for all our members.
  2. Street, bus stop and public Human Rights lectures every two weeks.
  3. Capacity building workshops for teachers, educationists, CBOS, women groups, professionals.
  4. Human Rights Advocacy and campaigns in Radio, Television and Newspapers etc.
  5. Human Rights Rally and Advocacy visit.
  6. Seminars, workshops, symposia, conferences for students, traders, unions etc.
  7. Publication of Human Rights Bulletins, Newsletters, Journals, Pamphlets, Handbooks, Books etc.
  8. Human Rights counseling, offering legal advice and litigation in courts.
  9. Human Rights investigation, Research, Monitoring and Documentation.
  10. Networking and collaboration with other like-minded organizations, and, with relevant United Nation Agencies, NGOS, Coalitions, CBOS, and local and International Organizations. To develop written visual materials, strategic pedagogues and methodologies which are culturally relevant including gender perspective to service the facilitation of programs in schools, institutions and communities that join to become agent of change.
