About People's Right Organization (PRO)

MOTTO: Upholding International Human Rights Standards.

BACKGROUND The vision of Peoples Rights Organization, PRO was received in 1993, while the founder, Dr. C. C. Nwadigo was a staff Attorney with Civil Liberties Organization, CLO, one of the foremost African NGOs. He had to undergo further and better training both in other Human Rights Organizations and even Bible School to receive more Divine Direction of the vision. The vision which is summarized as empowering African people with knowledge of their Rights to enable them maximize their potentials and live in Dignity never started until January, 1998 when the urge to start became irresistible. PRO started in the founders Law office in Lagos, at No. 53 Western Avenue Ojuelegba, Surulere, Lagos - Nigeria in company of Pastor Charles Ikoh; a journalist and another fellow:

TARGET AUDIENCE: The general public at all levels of literacy and education, ignorant people, traders, the poor, businessmen, women, children, youths, students, militant groups, Electorates/voters, police, politicians, professionals, prison officials, teachers, curriculum developers, judges, lawyers, armed forces, the media, parliamentarians, government officials, appropriate civil society e.g. Community based organizations, CBOS, NGOS, organized labour unions, women groups and other groups well placed the ensure.

STANDARDS: The standard for PRO's work is Human Rights Standards as provided for in the United Nations (UN) Charter, and International (UN) and Regional Human Rights Instruments, Declarations, Conventions, Covenants, Resolutions and Laws and Constitutions of the respective African States. We are guided by Human Rights values, norms, tenets and principles.

THE REASONS FOR FOUNDING THE ORGANIZATION

  • (A) Considering the long and protracted era of military dictatorship and sit tight leaders in Africa which was characterized by encouragement of illiteracy, ignorance, mass poverty, celebration of mediocrity resulting to Culpable mis-governance and gross underdevelopment of the continent.
  • (B) Regretting people’s ignorance of their basic Human Rights which is not taught as a subject in Primary, Secondary and even Tertiary Institutions except in few Law faculties, hence Human Rights is seen wrongly as purely a legal concept.
  • (C) Regretting also that the long period of dictatorial and oppressive reign of the Juntas and Cabals which have adversely affected the psyche of the people of Africa to think that full respect for Human Rights and Fundamental Freedoms as well as good governance is no longer possible in our generation.
  • (D) Understanding that Human Rights provisions in the Constitutions, Charters, Conventions and Declarations are designed to benefit and protect the people against coercive and oppressive governmental authority.
  • (E) Knowing fully well that empowerment of the people would drastically reduce incessant Human Rights violations and break the vicious cycle of dictatorship in Africa.
  • (F) Deeply concerned with the absence of Peace and Human Rights Education as well as ethical and moral Education in the school curricula in Africa which may have contributed to reckless disregard for Human lives and Dignity and devastation of the continent by ethnic/local militia, armed bandits, violent youths and violent rebels without cause resulting into mindless killings, mass murder, genocide etc, on account of intolerance, ignorance, primitivity etc.
  • (G) Welcoming the need to Reform obsolete and anachronistic Laws in the statutes books in order to make our domestic Laws, Human Rights compliant and International Law friendly.
  • (H) Considering also the fact that existing Human Rights Organizations have maintained exclusive membership and concentrated on Board room seminars, workshops etc of an elitist in nature, which have failed to empower majority of the African people.
  • (I) Lamenting also the unmistakable presence of absolute and extreme poverty, misery, squalid surroundings, widespread diseases, starvation, severe deprivation of basic amenities of life coupled with perennial unemployment afflicting majority of the people of the Continent, while political leaders are engaged in wanton looting, unbridled corruption and primitive acquisition of wealth.
  • (J) Recognizing the need to develop strong, virile and vibrant civil society that would know their Rights and responsibilities to hold the elected representatives accountable to fulfil their aspirations, while defending democracies in Africa.
  • (K) Considering the continued discrimination and violence against women and the girl child, partly attributed to obnoxious customs and traditions which have hampered development of women's potentials and retarded economic progress and prosperity of the Continent.
  • (L) Lamenting also the devastation and pollution of the natural environment and violation of people's Rights to Clean and healthy environment.
  • (M) Emphasizing the need to re-orientate, sensitize and engage in consciousness raising Human Rights Education and empowerment of the people with knowledge of their civil, political, social, economic and cultural Rights to enable secure quality life of Human Dignity.
  • (N) Emphasizing also the fact that unless and until the people for whom and in whose interest Human Rights exist, know, assert, and defend their Rights, as contained in various Charters, Covenant, Conventions, Declarations and the Constitutions would remain dead letters thereat.
